Privacy Policy

Bulk Actions Hub for Zoho Desk · Publisher: Atesen Software · Contact: support@atesensoftware.com · Last updated: 2 October 2026

What the extension does

Bulk Actions Hub runs inside your Zoho Desk account. It reads tickets, agents, views, ticket fields and From addresses of the department you open; and — only after a user previews and confirms — updates tickets, adds or removes tags, moves tickets and sends email replies through the Zoho Desk API.

Data we collect

None. Atesen Software operates no server for this extension. All requests go from the user's browser to the Zoho Desk API through Zoho's own connection and authorization. We do not receive, store, sell or share your ticket, contact or account data. The extension contains no analytics or tracking code.

Data stored in your Zoho Desk

  • Presets you save (name, filters, changes and reply text) are stored in the extension's data storage inside your Zoho Desk organization, visible to users of the extension in your portal.
  • Run history: date, agent name, number of tickets changed/failed, the fields changed and the filter summary. No ticket content.
  • If Zoho storage is unavailable, presets and history are kept in your browser's local storage instead.
  • Installation settings (allow changes, allow replies, max tickets per run) are stored by Zoho Desk.

All of this is removed by uninstalling the extension or clearing the browser storage. Changes made to tickets are recorded in Zoho Desk's own ticket history under the agent who ran them.

Undo files

Undo and result files (CSV) contain ticket IDs, numbers, subjects and old/new field values. They are created in the browser and saved to the user's own computer. We never receive them.

Permissions

The extension requests these Zoho Desk scopes: read and update tickets, create (tags on) tickets, search, read basic settings (departments, agents, views, fields) and read email channel settings (From addresses). It uses them only for the features above. It does not request delete permissions.

Third parties

The extension loads Zoho's own extension SDK from Zoho's servers. It loads no other external scripts, fonts or services.

Payments

Purchases and billing are handled by Zoho Marketplace. We do not receive your payment details.

Support communications

If you email us, we keep your email address and message only to answer you, and delete support threads 24 months after the last message.

Your rights

Because we hold no data from the extension, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For support emails, write to support@atesensoftware.com and we will send you a copy or delete them.

Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
