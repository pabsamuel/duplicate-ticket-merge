Changelog

Duplicate Ticket Merge for Zoho Desk

0.1.0 — private beta (21 September 2026)

  • Duplicates panel on the ticket detail page: same contact, same subject, or same contact + similar subject.
  • Department scan (top band): 7, 14 or 30 days, up to 1,000 tickets per scan, duplicate groups with checkboxes.
  • Preview before every merge; typing MERGE is required above 20 tickets.
  • Merges in batches that respect Zoho Desk's merge limit; pause, resume and cancel.
  • Private audit note on the master ticket.
  • Settings: matching window, similarity threshold, include closed tickets, allow merging.
